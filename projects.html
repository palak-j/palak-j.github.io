BIOMEDICAL NLP / PUBLISHED RESEARCH

Sjögren’s Syndrome Causality Extraction

A transfer-learning-based NLP pipeline to identify and organize causal relationships in biomedical literature. The work applies relation and entity extraction to investigate factors discussed in research on Sjögren’s syndrome.
