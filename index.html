01 / A LITTLE CONTEXT

A bit about my work.

My work has mostly been around images, text, and other data that doesn't come neatly organized. I've worked in media analysis, built machine-learning models, and spent time on NLP research. The problems are often less about picking a model and more about understanding the data and what a useful result actually looks like.

I like the parts of data science that require a little digging: understanding why a model behaves a certain way, testing whether an improvement is real, and working through practical constraints. I'm also interested in how the field is changing as generative AI becomes part of everyday tooling.

Lately, I've been following multimodal models, model evaluation, image authenticity, and practical ways to use LLMs without treating every problem as a chatbot.
